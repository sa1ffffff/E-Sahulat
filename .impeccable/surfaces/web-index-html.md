Scope: the whole E-Sahulat web app (Ask, Knowledge base, Evaluation). Mode: Operate.
Audience: Pakistani citizens with a passport question, often on phones, in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu; secondary: university evaluators who should be impressed at first look and able to inspect decisions and metrics.
Task: ask, read the decision and the verbatim official quote, follow the source; browse passages; read measured results.
Constraints: no fee schedule implied; stand-in embedder disclosed; Urdu RTL Nastaliq; runs locally.
Memorable moment: the attestation stamp landing on the answer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every answer is a photocopy of the official line, attested as a true copy. Refuses the chat-bubble assistant: there is no conversation thread, only a question and an attested document.

OWN-WORLD: Photocopy-white ground (#f3f3f0) with toner-gray rules; a bottle-green file-cover shell (#1d4a37); quotes set in a gazette serif on paper strips with a faint toner edge; rubber-stamp inks carry decisions: violet (verified), ochre (further particulars required), red (not on record). Archivo for UI, condensed heavy caps for stamps, Noto Nastaliq Urdu for Urdu.

STORY: The citizen asks in any script, sees one clear stamped outcome, reads the exact official words in English and Urdu, and taps the source. Evaluators open the trace and the measured results.
