Can a child under 15 get a ten-year passport?
Children under 15 years of age will be issued Passport having 05 years validity only.

15 سال سے کم عمر بچوں کو صرف 05 سال کی مدت والا پاسپورٹ جاری کیا جائے گا۔

Source F12 · DGI&P Ordinary Passport · Citation verified
